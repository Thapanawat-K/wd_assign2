Our Restaurants

Six handpicked spots in Melbourne's CBD.

Dainty Sichuan Noodles QV

Chinese / Sichuan

A quick and casual noodle spot tucked away in Red Cape Lane at QV. Dainty Sichuan specialises in spicy and savoury rice noodle dishes straight from the heart of Sichuan, with generous portions and an easy QR-code ordering system perfect for a fast city lunch.

Signature dishes:

  • Chongqing Spicy Noodle with Beef - $18
  • Signature Sichuan Lamb Chop Rice Noodle Soup - $22
  • Sichuan Cold Noodle - $14

Average price: $20 per person

Reservation deposit: No deposit required

DoDee Paidang Swanston

Thai

Authentic Thai street food in a lively, modern setting on Swanston Street. Famous for their signature Tom Yum noodles served at multiple spice levels, DoDee Paidang serves up bold flavours, friendly service, and even has robot waiters that bring the food to your table.

Signature dishes:

  • Tom Yum Noodles - $18
  • Soft Shell Crab Pad Thai - $24
  • Stewed Pork Leg Rice - $20

Average price: $22 per person

Reservation deposit: No deposit required

Reserve

Yappari Steak Swanston

Japanese Steakhouse

Originating from Okinawa, Yappari Steak serves sizzling rare steaks on hot Mt Fuji lava stone plates, letting you cook the meat to your preferred doneness right at the table. Customise with over 10 sauces and pair with garlic rice, miso soup, or one of their crispy croquettes.

Signature dishes:

  • Oyster Blade Steak (200g) - $26
  • Wagyu Momo Steak (200g) - $38
  • Beef Tendon Curry Rice - $18

Average price: $35 per person

Reservation deposit: $20

Soi 38

Thai Street Food

A cult Melbourne favourite famous for being the first to bring authentic Bangkok-style boat noodles to the city. Now in Tivoli Arcade, Soi 38 serves fragrant broths, papaya salads, crying tiger beef, and grilled pork skewers in a vibrant atmosphere that channels real Thai street food energy.

Signature dishes:

  • Bangkok-Style Boat Noodles - $15
  • Pad Krapow with Pork - $18
  • Crying Tiger Beef Brisket - $22

Average price: $25 per person

Reservation deposit: No deposit required

Gopals

Vegetarian / Vegan

Established in 1980, Gopals is Melbourne's oldest vegetarian restaurant, run by Hare Krishna Melbourne on Swanston Street. The rotating menu blends Indian, European, and Asian influences, with affordable feast meals featuring curries, salads, samosas, lasagne, and signature desserts.

Signature dishes:

  • Feast Meal (rice, 2 curries, 2 salads, drink, dessert) - $14
  • Eggplant Lasagne - $13
  • Samosa with Tomato Chutney - $5

Average price: $15 per person

Reservation deposit: No deposit required

Udon Yasan

Japanese / Udon

A no-frills self-service udon spot on Bourke Street, inspired by Japan's casual noodle shops. Freshly made udon noodles in flavourful broth, with plenty of free toppings (spring onion, seaweed, tempura flakes) and tempura sides. A favourite for budget-friendly, satisfying lunches.

Signature dishes:

  • Sukiyaki Beef Udon - $10.40
  • Beef Curry Udon - $11.50
  • Kimchi Udon - $10.90

Average price: $12 per person

Reservation deposit: No deposit required
